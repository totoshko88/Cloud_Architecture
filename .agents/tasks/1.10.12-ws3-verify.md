# 1.10.12 WS3 — crossing-aware contact selection (C7): verification

Plan: `.agents/tasks/1.10.12-engine-plan.md` §1.2 and WS3. Metrics: `.agents/tasks/1.10.12-metrics.md` → *WS3*.

## What changed

1. **Face variants in the scored search** (`layout/variants.py::_face_variants`). Back-edges (loop-above / descend-near) and converging runs (`fan-out-row` / `cross-region`, lane-above / lane-below) get the other legal entry face (left ↔ top) as extra variants, flagged `face_swap`. They are ranked after every corridor family and left out of the most-constrained-first ordering key (`generate(..., faces=False)`), so the routing order of every edge is unchanged. The solver (`layout/solver.py::solve`) admits a face swap only on **strictly fewer crossings** with rule errors and warnings no worse; it never takes one for rails, turns or ink. Without that gate the search took a face swap on SEP 03 for a shorter line, and the finished diagram got worse (2 → 4 crossings, one own-caption cut). Spines get no extra variant: their family already varies both faces (straight-drop, right-gap, left-gap). A right-corridor → left-entry spine was tried and dropped, because on the HA summary the strict layout then raised (`corridor-sharing` s4/s9 the repair loop could not separate). Exits keep their rule-based face: a bottom exit from a captioned source strikes its caption (Variant A, WS2).
2. **Converging corridor nesting by source position** (`layout/routers.py::decide_converging_turns`, read by `route_fan_out_row` through `CorridorAllocator.turn_rank`). Fan-outs into one target's left face are handed their turn columns by the source's distance from its entry row: the farther source turns nearer the target. Before, the columns followed the declared order. No corpus or SEP diagram declared such a group the wrong way round, so this changes no output today. SEP 01 `e4`/`e5` is hand-routed and is fixed by the repair pass's corridor swap.
3. **Final crossing-repair pass** (`crossing_repair.py`, called from `diagram_layout._plan_from_text` after the WS2 caption-safe plan and merged into the same `CaptionPlan`, so the HA multi-skin precomputed plan gets it too). For every crossing pair, three moves are tried in order: a corridor swap of two edges converging on one face (or diverging from one), a single re-route of either edge over every free legal exit/entry face pair (stub + one-lane shapes plus the WS2 side-corridor shapes), and a joint re-route of the pair, or of the pair plus one edge that shares an endpoint. Candidates go through the WS2 cheap admissibility checks. A move is kept **only** when the crossing count drops, no error, warning, rail, collinear pair, own-caption cut or visible strike is added, and no finding absent before appears (set comparison).
4. **Lint `edge-crossing-avoidable`** (WARNING, both classes; `linter.py`, documented in `.kiro/steering/diagram-lint.md`). It uses `crossing_repair.avoidable_crossings`, the same test as the repair pass and the `Xav` metric. `diagram-standards.md` gains *Crossing-aware contact selection (1.10.12)* and a checklist line.
5. `scripts/canvas_metrics.py`: `Xav` = the shared definition; the WS0 definition is kept as `Xraw`.
