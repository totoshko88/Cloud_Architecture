## What changed

1. **Edges beneath vertices (z-order).** `diagram_layout._edge_slots` places each edge on the root layer before every boundary, or as the first child of the last *filled* boundary its polyline touches. A child edge's waypoints are written relative to that boundary (`edge_cell(parent=, origin=)`), and absolute geometry is unchanged. Before this, every edge was emitted after the boundaries, so it painted above the nodes and `labelBackgroundColor` never masked a line. A node whose caption a beneath-edge crosses gets the local container fill as its caption background. `orthogonalise_text` / `edge_hygiene_text` write points relative to the edge's parent, so the hygiene passes stay correct. The old `_own_caption_cuts` mitigation is removed.
2. **Variant A (D3 / G15).** New module `rule_engine/caption_routing.py`. `build_diagram` runs it as a finishing pass on the assembled document, judged by the linter's own geometry. For every edge that crosses its own source or target caption (`geometry.check_edge_crosses_own_label`), it searches contract-legal alternatives:
   - Search shapes: a side corridor (exit right past the caption overhang, enter the target top/left), a prefix swap onto the route's own lanes, a leg shift, and a re-entry.
   - Joint moves: re-routes together with 1 or 2 partner edges the oracle names. This is a bounded greedy compatible assignment.
   - Container growth: the box grows right/down, with its ancestors and the right-margin Flow/Legend, at most 60 px, and is rejected if it adds an overlap or dead-space finding.
   - Overloaded source (three other right exits): the bottom exit moves into a corner band outside the caption's real text extent (decision D).
   - Acceptance: a candidate is kept only if the cut goes and nothing the oracle or the route-quality ratchet measures rises (errors, warnings incl. caption/detour/marker extras, crossings, rails, rail penalty, collinear pairs vs plan start, own, vis).
   - Visit order: the plan runs in declared order, plus the reverse order when it is still needed or the diagram has at most 12 edges.
   - The layout oracle serializes with `caption_safe=False`.
   - The HA builders compute one plan on the representative presentation (widest node labels, AWS caption styles) and apply it to all four skins, so geometry parity holds (`build_diagram(caption_plan=…)`, `caption_safe_plan`).
3. **Lint.** `edge-crosses-label` gains reason `edge-crosses-own-label` (WARNING). New geometry helpers: `check_edge_crosses_own_label`, `caption_text_box`, `check_edge_strikes_own_text`, `collinear_edge_pairs` (moved from `canvas_metrics`, unchanged), and `container_caption_geom` / `polyline_cuts_container_caption`, refactored out of `check_edge_crosses_container_label` with the same verdicts.
4. **Metrics.** `canvas_metrics.py` gains `vis` (visible strikes against the real text extent).
5. **Tests.** New `tests/test_ws2_caption_strikes.py` (7 tests). Tests that pinned the pre-Variant-A bottom drops were re-baselined to the Variant A invariants: `test_genai_hub_routing` (2), `test_geometry_hard_rules` (2), and `test_route_cost_properties` (a rail-free landscape is accepted). `test_lint_tightening_1_10_7` sets aside the new reason and adds `test_corpus_own_caption_strikes_only_on_overloaded_faces`. `test_marker_on_edge_1_10_7` uses `caption_safe=False` for its fixed-route marker test.
6. **Corpus.** Regenerated through the engine (`scripts/regen_examples.py`, never hand-edited), and all 17 rasters re-exported.

## Rework for review B1 (wrapped captions on overloaded faces)

The decision came from the orchestrator: option 1, which amends decision 2. When a source is overloaded (three other right exits, so the bottom exit is unavoidable) and its one-line caption is wider than the icon, the caption wraps onto two lines. Fallback 3 applies per node. Option B (a 4th right exit) stays rejected. Restated criterion: 0 VISIBLE strikes, and any geometric own-cut that remains is on an overloaded face and listed with its reason.

### Changes
- `geometry`:
  - New `wrap_caption`: breaks at a space or after a hyphen, nearest the middle, never inside a word.
  - New `caption_band`: one-line band + 16 px (`CAPTION_LINE_H`) per further line.
  - New `node_footprint`.
  - `node_caption_box` and `caption_text_box` now use `caption_band`. The old height was 16 px per line with a 30 px floor; no corpus or SEP caption had more than one line, so no existing verdict moves.
  - Every check that measures a caption now uses the real wrapped height: container padding, node overlap, dead space, the icon-width label band of `edge-crosses-label`, and legend placement. Legend placement goes through `node_caption_box`.
- `caption_routing`:
  - When a corner exit finds no position outside the text, `_wrap_move` wraps the source caption and re-plans every edge of that node that then cuts its caption.
  - The result is kept only if three conditions hold against the UNWRAPPED diagram: nothing the oracle measures rises (errors, warnings, crossings, rails, collinear); own/vis drop; and padding, overlap and legend findings do not rise.
  - `CaptionPlan.wraps` records the wrapped nodes.
- `diagram_layout`:
  - `_apply_wraps` wraps each node's own label, so the HA skins each wrap at their own break.
  - `_render_node` writes the line break as `&#10;`. A raw newline in an XML attribute is normalised to a space by every parser, and the fixture builders use renderers that do not escape.
- Tests:
  - 4 new tests in `tests/test_ws2_caption_strikes.py`: wrap rules, wrapped footprint, the wrap on an overloaded wide caption, and the no-break fallback.
  - `test_node_caption_box_is_text_wide_and_centred` expects the new 3-line height, 30 + 2 × 16.
